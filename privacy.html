Privacy Policy

ShortHistory Publisher is a personal tool that uploads the owner's own history videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What it accesses. Only the account that the owner signs in with, and only to upload videos and read the account name.

What it stores. Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server of ours, and we do not operate any server.

Sharing. We do not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone.

Contact. Open an issue at github.com/yassineis/shorthistory.
